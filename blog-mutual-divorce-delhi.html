Mutual Divorce in Delhi 2026 — Process & Timeline

Mutual consent divorce ke complete steps. 6-18 mahine ka process, kya documents, kitni fees, kab pehli motion, kab dusri motion, alimony & child custody — Delhi family court ka expert guide.

Mutual Divorce Kya Hai?

Mutual Divorce (Mutual Consent Divorce) ek aisa legal process hai jisme dono partners aapasi sehmati se shaadi todne ka decision lete hain. Yeh contested divorce se much faster aur less stressful hota hai. Hindu couples ke liye Hindu Marriage Act 1955 — Section 13B applicable hai, aur civil/inter-religion couples ke liye Special Marriage Act 1954 — Section 28.

Delhi mein mutual divorce Family Courts mein file hota hai — Karkardooma, Rohini, Saket, Patiala House, ya Tis Hazari Family Courts mein. Petition couple jointly file karte hain.

Mutual Divorce Eligibility

  • Couple ki shaadi ko minimum 1 year ho gaya ho
  • Couple 1 year ya zyada se separately reh rahe ho (separation period)
  • Dono partners willing hon — koi force nahi
  • Couple is conclusion par pahonche hon ki marriage breakdown ho gaya hai aur saath rehna possible nahi

Two-Motion Process Explained

Mutual divorce mein 2 main court appearances hote hain — pehli motion aur dusri motion (final motion). Beech mein 6 months ka mandatory cooling-off period hota hai.

Motion 1 — First Motion / Joint Petition Filing

Couple advocate ke through joint petition Family Court mein file karte hain. Petition mein yeh declarations hote hain:

  • Hum dono ki shaadi ko 1+ saal ho gaye
  • Hum 1+ saal se separately reh rahe hain
  • Hum saath nahi reh sakte aur shaadi todne par sehmat hain
  • Yeh decision free will se liya hai, koi pressure nahi

Pehli appearance par couple court ke saamne statements record karte hain. Court applications accept karta hai. 6-month cooling-off period shuru hota hai.

6-Month Cooling-Off Period

Yeh period legally mandatory hai (kuch cases mein waiver possible hai — Supreme Court Amardeep Singh v. Harveen Kaur, 2017). Iss period mein:

  • Couple reconcile kar sakte hain (decision change kar sakte hain)
  • Counseling sessions ho sakte hain (court-recommended)
  • Mediation through court mediation centers possible
  • Agar reconciliation nahi hota, dusri motion ki taraf badhte hain

Motion 2 — Second Motion / Final Decree

6 months ke baad couple court mein wapas aate hain. Final statements record hote hain. Court satisfied ho jaata hai ki decision firm hai aur reconciliation possible nahi. Divorce Decree issue hota hai — yeh final hai aur 30 days ke appeal period ke baad enforceable.

Cooling-Off Period Waiver — Same Day Possible?

Supreme Court (Amardeep Singh v. Harveen Kaur, 2017) ne ruling di hai ki cooling-off period waive ho sakta hai agar:

  • Couple 1.5 years+ separately reh rahe ho already
  • All issues (alimony, child custody, property) settled hain
  • Reconciliation efforts already failed
  • Continuation of cooling-off period sirf delay hai aur kisi ko fayda nahi

Iss case mein motion 1 + motion 2 same day ya within weeks hone possible hai — total time 3-4 months reduce ho sakta hai.

Documents Required for Mutual Divorce

Mutual Divorce Documents

  • Marriage Certificate — original + certified copy
  • Aadhaar Cards (both spouses)
  • PAN Cards (both)
  • Address Proof — Delhi residence (for Delhi family court jurisdiction)
  • Photographs — 4-6 each, passport size
  • Joint Petition — drafted by advocate (3 affidavits + statements)
  • Settlement Agreement — alimony, child custody, property division
  • Income Proof — salary slips, ITR (for alimony calculation)
  • Children's Birth Certificates (if applicable)
  • Bank Statements (last 6 months)

Mutual Divorce Cost in Delhi

  • Court Filing Fee — ₹1,000-₹2,000
  • Stamp Paper / Affidavits — ₹1,000-₹1,500
  • Advocate Fee (humari service) — ₹25,000-₹50,000 (case complexity ke hisaab se)
  • Settlement Agreement Drafting — Included in advocate fee
  • Total Mutual Divorce Cost — ₹30,000-₹55,000 approximately

Alimony & Maintenance in Mutual Divorce

Mutual divorce mein alimony amount couple jointly decide karte hain — usually settlement agreement mein clearly mentioned hota hai. Factors:

  • Duration of marriage — longer marriage = higher alimony usually
  • Income disparity — higher earning spouse provides support
  • Standard of living during marriage
  • Children — child custody se related expenses
  • Spouse's career sacrifices — homemaker spouse to fair compensation

Forms: Lump-sum payment, monthly maintenance, or property transfer. Court usually couple's mutual agreement accept karta hai unless unfair.

Child Custody in Mutual Divorce

Child custody settlement agreement ka critical part hai. Forms:

  1. Sole Custody — ek parent ko exclusive custody, dusre ko visitation rights
  2. Joint Custody — dono parents share physical/legal custody
  3. Split Custody — multiple children ke case mein different parents ko

Court "best interest of child" principle dekhta hai. Kuch important factors:

  • Child's age, gender, preferences (12+ ke liye preferences considered)
  • Each parent's financial & emotional ability
  • Stability of home environment
  • Education continuity

Mutual Divorce vs Contested Divorce

  • Time: Mutual 6-18 months. Contested 3-7 years.
  • Cost: Mutual ₹30-55k. Contested ₹1-3 lakh+.
  • Stress: Mutual low. Contested high (cross-examinations, evidence, witnesses).
  • Privacy: Mutual private (closed court). Contested potentially public allegations.
  • Outcome: Mutual = certain. Contested = court ki marzi.
  • Children Impact: Mutual minimal. Contested significant emotional toll.

Recommendation: Agar reconciliation possible nahi hai, mutual divorce hi best route hai. Hum mediation karwa ke contested ko mutual mein convert karne mein bhi expert hain.

FAQs

Q1. Mutual divorce ke baad re-marry kab kar sakte hain?

Final divorce decree ke 30-90 din baad (appeal period over). Hamari court marriage service available hai second marriage ke liye.

Q2. Court marriage waale couples ka divorce alag rule hai?

Haan — Special Marriage Act 1954 ke under court marriage ki hui shaadi ka divorce SMA Section 28 ke under. Process mostly similar hai but applicable law alag.

Q3. Wife / husband out of India hai to mutual divorce possible hai?

Haan — power of attorney ya video conferencing ke through. NRI mutual divorce specially common hai. Hum yeh process handle karte hain.

Q4. Property division kaise hota hai?

Settlement agreement mein clearly likha jaata hai — kya property kis spouse ki, kya joint, kya divide. Court agreement accept karta hai unless unfair.
