Arya Samaj Marriage vs Court Marriage — Konsa Best?

Delhi mein Hindu / Sikh / Jain / Buddhist couples ke liye Arya Samaj ₹3,100 vs Court Marriage ₹6,100 — time, privacy, validity, eligibility ka complete comparison. 2 minute mein decide karein konsa rasta best hai.

Quick Decision Matrix

Agar aap busy hain aur sirf decision chahiye, yeh quick rule hai:

  • Hindu / Sikh / Jain / Buddhist couple + same-day chahiye + privacy chahiye → Arya Samaj Marriage ₹3,100
  • Inter-religion couple (Hindu-Muslim, Hindu-Christian, etc.) → Court Marriage ₹6,100 (only legal option)
  • NRI / Foreign couple → Court Marriage (worldwide accepted)
  • Visa / Embassy ke liye urgent → Court Marriage (Delhi Government certificate)

Detailed comparison neeche dekhte hain — har factor pe.

1. Eligibility — Kaun Kar Sakta Hai?

Arya Samaj Marriage

Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 ke under conduct hoti hai. Eligible: Hindu, Sikh, Jain, Buddhist couples (yeh sab Hindu Marriage Act ke under count hote hain). Inter-religion couples (Hindu-Muslim, Hindu-Christian) Arya Samaj nahi kar sakte.

Court Marriage

Special Marriage Act, 1954 ke under hoti hai. Eligible: kisi bhi religion ke couple — Hindu, Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Parsi, Jain, Buddhist, ya inter-religion combinations. Foreign nationals bhi eligible hain.

2. Time — Kitni Der Lagti Hai?

Arya Samaj Marriage — 1-2 Ghante

Sabse fast option. Subah aao, dopehar tak certificate. Process: Documents verification (20 min) → Hawan ceremony with 7 pheras (30-45 min) → Certificate preparation (15-20 min). Total: 1-2 ghante.

Court Marriage — Same Day to 30 Days

Standard process: 30-day notice period mandatory. Tatkal/Same-day option bhi available hai (humari premium service). Practical breakdown:

  • Standard Court Marriage: 30 din notice + 1 din ceremony = ~31 din
  • Tatkal Court Marriage: Same day, 2-3 ghante (₹12,000)

Detailed: 30-Day Notice Period — Skip Karne Ke Tarike.

3. Cost Comparison

Direct Cost Comparison

  • Arya Samaj Marriage — ₹3,100 all-inclusive (pandit, hawan, certificate, affidavit)
  • Court Marriage (Standard) — ₹6,100 all-inclusive (registration, affidavits, certificate)
  • Court Marriage (Tatkal Same-Day) — ₹12,000 (premium urgency service)
  • NRI Court Marriage — ₹7,000 (with embassy NOC guidance)

4. Privacy — Ghar Walon Ko Pata Chalega?

Arya Samaj — 100% Private

Koi notice nahi bheja jaata. Ghar walon ko bilkul nahi pata chalta jab tak couple khud na batayein. Same-day private ceremony, instant certificate.

Court Marriage — Notice on Court Board (Not Home)

30-day notice Rohini Court / Tis Hazari Court ke notice board par display hota hai (public). Lekin yeh notice aapke ghar par physically nahi jaata. Practical reality — ghar walon ko sirf tab pata chalta hai jab koi specifically court ke notice board check kare. Tatkal option mein notice issue itna prominent nahi hota.

Inter-caste / family opposition cases ke liye Inter-Caste Love Marriage Delhi blog mein detailed privacy strategies di hain.

5. Validity — Worldwide Acceptance

Arya Samaj Certificate

Domestic use ke liye fully valid — Hindu Marriage Act 1955 ke under recognized. Indian passport, Aadhaar update, bank joint account, insurance — sab jagah accepted. Best practice: Arya Samaj ke baad court mein registration karwa lo (combo) — phir worldwide validity bhi mil jaati hai.

Court Marriage Certificate

Worldwide accepted — Delhi Government ka official certificate. Visa applications, embassy verification, foreign property registration, international banking — har jagah valid. NRI couples ke liye yeh mandatory option hai. Court Marriage service par detail.

6. Religious vs Civil

Arya Samaj = religious + civil. Hawan, mantras, 7 pheras, sindoor, mangalsutra — full Hindu wedding ceremony. Religious sentiment retain hota hai.

Court Marriage = pure civil. Koi pandit, koi ceremony nahi. Sirf legal declaration aur signatures. Fast, no-frills.

7. Best Combo — Arya Samaj + Court Registration

Bahut couples yeh combo choose karte hain:

  1. Day 1: Arya Samaj Marriage (₹3,100) — religious ceremony + Arya Samaj certificate, same day, private
  2. Later: Court Registration of Arya Samaj Marriage (₹3,500 add-on) — Delhi Govt certificate jo worldwide valid hai

Total: ₹6,600 for both. Best of both worlds — religious satisfaction + legal worldwide validity. NRI couples ke liye yeh combo specially recommend karte hain.

FAQs

Q1. Arya Samaj certificate court marriage ke liye use ho sakta hai?

Haan — Arya Samaj certificate ke base par court mein registration of marriage karwa sakte hain. Bina 30-day notice ke. Cost: ₹3,500 add-on (kuch cases mein).

Q2. Hindu-Muslim couple Arya Samaj kar sakte hain?

Nahi. Arya Samaj sirf Hindu/Sikh/Jain/Buddhist couples ke liye hai. Inter-religion ke liye Court Marriage (Special Marriage Act) only option hai.

Q3. NRI couple ke liye konsa best?

Court Marriage — kyunki worldwide validity chahiye. NRI marriage service ₹7,000.

Q4. Arya Samaj certificate Indian passport mein name change ke liye chalti hai?

Haan, RPO (Regional Passport Office) Arya Samaj certificate accept karta hai for spouse name addition.
