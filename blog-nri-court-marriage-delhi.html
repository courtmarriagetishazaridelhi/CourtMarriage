Step-by-Step NRI Court Marriage Process Delhi

Step 1 — Pre-Arrival Planning (1-2 Months Before India Visit)

India aane se pehle humse WhatsApp par contact karein. Hum aapke documents ki list bhej denge. Agar abroad partner hai to:

  • Single Status Certificate (No Impediment Certificate / Certificate of No Marriage) — apne country ke local government / registry office se lein
  • UK: General Register Office ya local council se
  • USA: Secretary of State ya State Vital Records Office se
  • Canada: Province ke Registry se
  • UAE: Ministry of Justice ya Notary Public se
  • Australia: Births, Deaths and Marriages Registry se

Apostille ya Indian Embassy attestation on the above documents mandatory hai — hum guide karte hain.

Step 2 — India Mein Arrival + Documents Submission

India pahunchte hi humse milein. Documents check hote hain:

NRI Couple Documents (Abroad Partner)

  • Valid Passport (all pages) — original + certified copy
  • Valid Visa / OCI card
  • Single Status Certificate — apostilled ya Indian Embassy attested
  • Address proof of country of residence (utility bill / bank statement)
  • 6 passport-size photos
  • Divorce decree / death certificate (if applicable)

Indian Partner Documents

  • Aadhaar Card (Delhi address hona chahiye)
  • PAN Card
  • Age proof (10th marksheet / birth cert / passport)
  • Address proof (Delhi)
  • 6 passport-size photos

Step 3 — Notice Filing (30 Days) OR Tatkal (Same Day)

Standard SMA 1954 mein 30-day notice hota hai. NRI couples ke liye limited India stay ek challenge hai. Solutions:

  • Tatkal NRI Marriage Service (₹12,000): Same-day registration — specific provisions ke under. NRI visa deadline cases ke liye.
  • Pre-filing: Indian partner pehle se notice file kar de (30 din pehle). NRI partner sirf solemnization day ke liye India aaye.
  • Remote filing: Power of Attorney ke through Indian partner notice file kar sakta hai agar NRI currently India mein nahi.

Step 4 — Solemnization aur Certificate

30 days baad (ya tatkal mein same day) — dono partners + 3 witnesses Marriage Officer ke saamne declaration sign karte hain. Certificate issue hota hai. Yeh certificate:

  • Delhi Government official Form-V certificate
  • Worldwide accepted
  • Embassy verification ke liye valid
  • Spouse visa application ke liye gold standard
  • Foreign property, banking, insurance — sab ke liye accepted

Apostille Certificate Kya Hota Hai aur Kyun Chahiye?

Apostille ek international authentication stamp hai jo Hague Convention ke under documents ko worldwide valid banata hai. NRI couples ke liye yeh 2 situations mein chahiye:

  1. Abroad ke documents India mein use karne ke liye: Single Status Certificate ko India mein present karne se pehle apostille ya Indian Embassy attestation zaroori hai.
  2. Indian marriage certificate abroad use karne ke liye: Delhi ka marriage certificate foreign country mein use karne ke liye Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) apostille chahiye.

MEA apostille cost: ₹1,500 per document (humari add-on service). 5-7 working days. Rush processing bhi available.

Country-Wise Single Status Certificate Guide

United Kingdom (UK)

Certificate of No Impediment to Marriage — Local Register Office (council) se milti hai. Indian High Commission London se attestation/apostille karao. Document valid for 6 months typically.

United States of America (USA)

Affidavit of Single Status — notarized by local notary + apostilled by Secretary of State of your state. Indian Consulate se attestation alternate.

Canada

Province ke vital statistics / registry office se certificate. Indian High Commission Ottawa ya respective Consulate se attestation.

UAE / Gulf

Ministry of Justice ya Notary Public se Single Status Declaration. Indian Embassy attestation + MOFA UAE attestation both required.

Australia

Births, Deaths and Marriages Registry se. DFAT apostille + Indian High Commission attestation.

NRI Marriage Certificate — Kahan Kahan Use Hoti Hai?

  • UK Spouse Visa (Marriage Visa): UKVI Indian marriage certificate accept karta hai — apostilled honi chahiye
  • USA CR-1 / IR-1 Spouse Visa: USCIS ke liye Indian court marriage certificate valid hai
  • Canada Spousal Sponsorship (PR): IRCC ke liye Delhi government certificate sufficient hai
  • Australia Partner Visa (subclass 309/100): Department of Home Affairs accept karta hai
  • UAE Residence Visa: Ministry of Interior ke liye attested certificate chahiye
  • Indian Passport — Spouse Name Addition: Directly possible after marriage certificate
  • Foreign Bank Account Joint: Most international banks India marriage certificate accept karte hain
  • Property Registration: India mein joint property ke liye

FAQs — NRI Court Marriage Delhi

Q1. Kya sirf 2-3 din India stay mein NRI marriage ho sakti hai?

Haan — tatkal service (₹12,000) mein same-day certificate possible hai. Aur agar Indian partner ne 30 din pehle notice file kar di ho, to solemnization day ke liye sirf ek din India rehna padta hai.

Q2. NRI ka India mein permanent address nahi hai — kya chalega?

NRI ke liye Indian partner ka Delhi address sufficient hai for jurisdiction. NRI partner ka foreign address documents mein accept hota hai.

Q3. Marriage ke baad UK spouse visa kab apply kar sakte hain?

Marriage certificate milte hi. Certificate apostille karwao (5-7 days), phir UKVI ko submit karo. Processing time 6-24 weeks (priority available). Hum MEA apostille tak guide karte hain.

Q4. Online ceremony possible hai? (COVID rule)

Nahi — SMA 1954 ke tahat both parties physical presence mandatory hai for solemnization. Sirf notice filing remotely possible hai via Power of Attorney.

Q5. NRI divorce decree valid hai India mein?

Foreign divorce decree India mein recognized hoti hai under specific conditions. Case-by-case analysis zaroori hai — call karein.
