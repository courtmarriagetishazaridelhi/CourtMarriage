Court Marriage Eligibility — Who Can Apply?

Special Marriage Act 1954 ke under court marriage karne ke liye yeh basic conditions poori honi chahiye:

  • Age: Groom ki minimum age 21 years, bride ki minimum age 18 years.
  • Mental Capacity: Dono partners sound mind mein hone chahiye aur willingly consent de rahe hon — without any coercion or fraud.
  • Marital Status: Dono currently unmarried hone chahiye. Agar previous marriage hai to legal divorce decree ya death certificate mandatory hai.
  • Prohibited Relationship: Couple prohibited blood relations mein nahi aana chahiye (with some exceptions for community customs).
  • Address Proof: Court marriage Delhi mein karne ke liye dono mein se kam-se-kam ek partner ka Delhi address proof chahiye (last 30+ days for jurisdiction).

Foreign nationals aur NRI couples bhi Special Marriage Act ke under court marriage kar sakte hain — bas additional documents (passport, embassy NOC, single status certificate) chahiye honge. Detailed comparison ke liye Arya Samaj vs Court Marriage blog padhein.

Court Marriage Process Step-by-Step

Delhi mein court marriage 4 main steps mein complete hoti hai:

Step 1 — Initial Consultation & Documents Verification

Sabse pehle hum aapke documents WhatsApp ya office visit ke through verify karte hain. Aadhaar address Delhi ka hai ya nahi, age proof valid hai ya nahi, photos sahi format mein hain ya nahi — yeh sab check hota hai. Yeh consultation 100% FREE hai. Office Address: Rohini Court Sec-14 par walk-in bhi welcome hai (no appointment needed).

Step 2 — Notice of Intended Marriage

Special Marriage Act ke under "Notice of Intended Marriage" Marriage Officer (SDM) ke paas file karna hota hai. Yeh notice 30 din ke liye public hota hai — Rohini Court ke notice board par display hota hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki aapke ghar par notice jaata hai. Ghar walon ko kuch nahi pata chalta jab tak woh khud court ke notice board check na karein.

Notice file karne ke liye humari team aapke saath SDM office jaati hai, application form fill karti hai, affidavits sign karwa kar submit kar deti hai. 30-day notice period kab skip ho sakti hai — yeh detail blog padhein.

Step 3 — 30-Day Waiting Period (or Same-Day for Tatkal)

Notice publish hone ke baad 30 din wait karna padta hai. Is period mein agar koi person valid legal objection raise karta hai (jaise pehli marriage abhi tak intact hai, age proof galat hai, etc.) to Marriage Officer inquiry karte hain. Practical reality — 99% cases mein koi objection nahi aata.

Tatkal Court Marriage: Agar aapko same-day certificate chahiye to tatkal court marriage service mein hum specific procedures use karte hain — Special Marriage Rules ke under jo emergency provisions hain. Cost ₹12,000 (regular ₹6,100 se zyada). NRI couples, visa urgency, foreign travel — yeh cases ke liye tatkal best hai.

Step 4 — Solemnization & Certificate Issue

30 din ke baad (ya tatkal mein same day) couple aur 3 witnesses Marriage Officer ke saamne declaration sign karte hain. Yeh declaration ke 3 statements:

  1. Hum dono apni marzi se shaadi kar rahe hain
  2. Hum dono adult hain aur eligible hain
  3. Hum dono prohibited relationship mein nahi hain

Marriage Officer dono partners aur witnesses ke signatures lekar Marriage Certificate (Form-V) issue karta hai. Yeh certificate Delhi Government ka official document hai — lifetime valid, worldwide accepted. Aap usi din certified copies le sakte hain.

Common Pitfalls & Mistakes to Avoid

9+ saal ke experience mein humne dekhi hain yeh common mistakes — aap avoid karein:

  1. Galat Address Proof: Aadhaar address agar Delhi ka nahi hai aur aap Delhi mein register karna chahte hain to delay hota hai. Solution: 1 month ka rent agreement banao Delhi address par.
  2. Mismatched Names: Aadhaar mein "Rajesh Kumar" aur PAN mein "R Kumar" — yeh mismatch reject kar sakta hai. Pehle Aadhaar/PAN update karein matching names ke liye.
  3. Witness Confusion: Witnesses ko Aadhaar + PAN dono chahiye. Couple ko apne taraf se 3 witnesses laane hote hain — family member, friend, ya colleague.
  4. Photo Format Mistakes: Court ke liye specific format ki photographs chahiye — color, plain background, recent (last 6 months). Old wedding photos chalti nahi.
  5. Affidavit Errors: Self-drafted affidavits often reject ho jaate hain. Hum ready-format affidavits bana ke dete hain — directly notary se sign karwa lo.

Court Marriage FAQs

Q1. Kya court marriage same day possible hai Delhi mein?

Haan — specific provisions ke under same-day court marriage Delhi mein possible hai, especially Rohini Court par. Iske liye tatkal court marriage service use karte hain. Cost regular se zyada hoti hai (₹12,000 vs ₹6,100), but emergency cases (visa, foreign travel, medical) ke liye perfect.

Q2. Court marriage karne ke baad ghar walon ko pata chalega?

Nahi, jab tak couple khud na batayein. Notice Rohini Court ke notice board par hota hai, aapke ghar par koi physical letter nahi jaata. Marriage certificate sirf couple ko issue hota hai. Inter-caste / love marriage mein privacy 100% maintained hoti hai.

Q3. Court marriage certificate kahan-kahan accepted hota hai?

Court Marriage Certificate (Form-V) worldwide accepted hai — passport application, visa, bank joint accounts, property registration, insurance, embassy verification — har jagah valid. Lifetime validity, kabhi expire nahi hota.

Q4. Religious ceremony zaroori hai?

Nahi — court marriage purely civil registration hai. Koi pandit, qazi, priest required nahi. Agar aap religious ceremony bhi karna chahte hain to Arya Samaj marriage + court registration combo bhi available hai.

Q5. Inter-caste ya inter-religion couples ke liye court marriage best hai?

Haan, court marriage (Special Marriage Act) hi inter-religion couples ke liye only legally recognized option hai. Dono partners apna religion retain kar sakte hain, koi conversion zaroori nahi. Detailed guide: Inter-Caste Love Marriage Delhi.

Q6. NRI couple ke liye court marriage process kya hai?

NRI couples ko additional documents chahiye — passport, embassy se NOC (Single Status Certificate / No Impediment Certificate), aur foreign passport copy notarized. Process same hai but timeline 1-2 din zyada lag sakti hai. Hamari NRI marriage service ₹7,000 mein embassy assistance bhi included hai.
