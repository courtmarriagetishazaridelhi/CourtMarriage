Hidden Charges — Watch Out For These

Delhi mein kuch consultants low pricing dikhate hain phir hidden charges add karte hain. Avoid yeh red flags:

  1. "Government Fee Extra" — Yeh actually ₹100-150 hai. Agar koi ₹2,000 charge kare to bogus.
  2. "Notice Publication Charge" — Court notice board free hota hai. Newspaper publication mandatory nahi hai.
  3. "Marriage Officer Fee" — Officer salary government deti hai. Direct payment illegal hai.
  4. "Witness Charges" — Couple ko apne witnesses laane hote hain. Agar koi consultant "witness arrangement" charge kare aur fake witnesses provide kare to illegal.
  5. "Express Processing Fee" — Tatkal ka official price hi yeh include karta hai.

Hum 100% transparent hain — jo price aap phone par sunoge, wahi pay karoge. Pricing page par sab clear hai.

  1. Hindu/Sikh/Jain/Buddhist couples: Arya Samaj (₹3,100) cheaper hai if domestic use only. Worldwide validity chahiye to court combo (₹6,600 total).
