Court Marriage Documents List 2026 — Delhi

Complete documents checklist for Court Marriage in Delhi under Special Marriage Act 1954. Aadhaar, PAN, age proof, photos, affidavits, witnesses — sab kuch detailed format mein, with NRI & divorcee special documents.

Call: 9891343962 WhatsApp

Mandatory Documents — Both Partners

Yeh saare documents dono partners ke liye separately chahiye honge:

Identity & Age Proof

  • Aadhaar Card — Original + 2 photocopies. Address Delhi ka hona chahiye for Delhi jurisdiction.
  • PAN Card — Original + 2 photocopies. Names should match Aadhaar.
  • Date of Birth Proof — Any one: 10th Class Certificate, Birth Certificate (Municipality issued), Passport, ya School Leaving Certificate.
  • Age Verification: Groom 21+, Bride 18+ mandatory under Special Marriage Act 1954.

Address Proof (Delhi)

  • Voter ID Card — most preferred
  • Driving License — Delhi issued
  • Electricity Bill / Water Bill / Gas Connection — last 3 months
  • Bank Passbook — recent entries
  • Rent Agreement — registered, minimum 1 month old (for outstation residents who recently moved to Delhi)

Note: Kam-se-kam ek partner ka Delhi address proof mandatory hai for Delhi court jurisdiction.

Photographs

  • Passport-size photographs — 6-8 copies each partner
  • Color photos, plain white background
  • Recent (last 6 months)
  • Both ears visible, no sunglasses, no headwear (except religious)
  • Joint photographs (couple together) — 4 copies

Witness Documents

Court Marriage ke liye 3 witnesses chahiye — couple ko apne taraf se laane hote hain (e.g. family member, friend, ya colleague). Har witness ke liye:

  • Aadhaar Card — original + photocopy
  • PAN Card — original + photocopy
  • 2 passport-size photos
  • Witnesses ko in person aana hota hai for declaration signing

Note: Hum sirf documentation aur legal procedure handle karte hain. Witnesses couple ki taraf se hone chahiye. Detailed: Court Marriage Process Guide.

Affidavits — What's Required

Court Marriage ke liye 3 affidavits draft karne padte hain (humari team aapke liye prepare karti hai):

  1. Marital Status Affidavit — confirming both partners are unmarried (or legally divorced/widowed)
  2. Age Affidavit — confirming both partners meet minimum age requirements
  3. Address Affidavit — confirming Delhi residence for jurisdiction

Affidavits stamp paper (₹10-₹50) par notarized hote hain. Format ready milti hai humare paas — bas couple sign karte hain notary ke saamne.

Special Cases — Additional Documents

1. Divorcee Couple

  • Final Divorce Decree — court-issued, original + certified copy
  • Mutual Consent Order (if applicable)
  • Decree should be at least 30 days old (appeal period over)

Detailed: Mutual Divorce Process Delhi.

2. Widow / Widower

  • Death Certificate of previous spouse — original + certified copy
  • Affidavit confirming widowhood

3. NRI / Foreign National

  • Valid Passport — all pages
  • Valid Visa (for foreign nationals)
  • Single Status Certificate / No Impediment Certificate — issued by home country embassy
  • Apostille / Embassy Attestation on foreign documents
  • Address proof of country of residence

NRI Marriage Service ₹7,000 — embassy NOC guidance included.

4. Inter-Religion Couple

Special Marriage Act 1954 use hota hai (Arya Samaj nahi). Same documents as standard court marriage. Religion conversion mandatory nahi hota — dono apna religion retain kar sakte hain. Detail: Inter-Caste Love Marriage Delhi.

Common Document Mistakes — Avoid These

  1. Name Mismatch: Aadhaar mein "Rajesh Kumar" aur PAN mein "R Kumar" — yeh reject ho sakta hai. Aadhaar/PAN update karwa lo first.
  2. Old Photos: 2 saal purane photos chalti nahi. Recent photos lo.
  3. Outdated Address: Voter ID address agar Delhi nahi hai aur aap Delhi mein register karna chahte hain to issue. Solution: Rent agreement ya electricity bill use karo.
  4. Witness Documents Missing: Witnesses ke Aadhaar + PAN dono mandatory.
  5. Foreign Documents Without Apostille: NRI couples ke liye apostille/embassy attestation skip nahi kar sakte.

Documents for Arya Samaj Marriage

Agar aap court marriage instead of Arya Samaj choose kar rahe hain, documents thoda kam hain:

  • Aadhaar Card (both)
  • DOB proof
  • Address proof
  • 6 photos each
  • 2 witnesses with Aadhaar (couple ki taraf se)
