Court Marriage Certificate Duplicate Process — Delhi

Agar aapki Special Marriage Act (SMA) court marriage Rohini Court ya Tis Hazari Court mein hui thi, to duplicate certificate usi office se milega.

Step 1 — FIR File Karein (Recommended)

Certificate lost/stolen hai to nearest police station mein General Diary (GD) ya FIR file karein — certificate lost ka reason document karne ke liye. Yeh mandatory nahi hai, but government offices isko prefer karte hain.

Step 2 — Affidavit Banwayein

Notary se ek affidavit banwayein: "My marriage certificate has been lost and I require a duplicate copy." Stamp paper (₹10-50) par. Hum yeh draft karte hain.

Step 3 — Application to Marriage Registrar

Jis SDM office mein marriage register hui thi — wahan application submit karein:

  • Application letter (marriage date, registration number mention karein agar pata ho)
  • Affidavit of loss
  • Aadhaar cards of both spouses (copies)
  • Any other proof of marriage (old photos, invitation, etc.)
  • Nominal fee (₹50-₹100 government side)

Step 4 — Record Verification aur Issuance

Marriage Registrar apne records mein check karta hai. Usually 3-7 working days mein certified copy issue hoti hai. Original certificate nahi milta — certified copy ya attested extract milta hai, jo legally equivalent hai.

Arya Samaj Marriage Certificate Duplicate

Agar Arya Samaj marriage ka certificate kho gaya hai, to Arya Samaj Mandir (jahan ceremony hui thi) se duplicate milega. Process:

  • Mandir ke in-charge se contact karein
  • Marriage date, groom/bride names, registration number batayen
  • Affidavit submit karein
  • Nominal fee: ₹100-₹500 (mandir ke hisaab se)
  • 3-5 working days mein copy milegi

Note: Agar bahut purana marriage hai (5+ years) ya mandir change ho gaya, to hum alternative route nikaalte hain — call karein.

Muslim Nikah Certificate Duplicate

Nikahnama ki duplicate copy ke liye:

  • Jis Qazi ne Nikah karaaya tha unse contact karein — unke paas copy hogi
  • Waqf Board ya Muslim Personal Law Board registered Qazi records se bhi milti hai
  • Agar court registration bhi hui thi (SMA ya Marriage Registration Act ke tahat) to above court process follow karein

Hindu Marriage Certificate Duplicate (HMA Registration)

Agar religious Hindu marriage (pandit-performed) ka court registration hua tha — Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) se certified copy milegi. Process same hai as court marriage above.

Kya Old Records Bhi Milte Hain?

Haan — Delhi Government marriage records 1969 se maintain kiye ja rahe hain (kuch cases mein aur purane bhi). Sirf registration number ya exact date pata ho to record trace ho sakta hai. Humara experience hai 20-30 saal purane records bhi dhoondhne ka.

FAQs

Q1. Registration number pata nahi hai, tab bhi milega?

Haan — marriage date aur full names ke basis par record trace hota hai. Thoda time zyada lag sakta hai.

Q2. Certified copy original ki jagah valid hai?

Haan — legally certified copy original ke barabar hai for passport, visa, bank, insurance — sab purposes ke liye.

Q3. Both spouses ka aana zaroori hai duplicate ke liye?

Ek spouse apply kar sakta hai. Dusre ka Aadhaar proof attach karna hota hai.

Q4. Online duplicate certificate milti hai Delhi mein?

Delhi government ka e-District portal (edistrict.delhigovt.nic.in) par kuch services available hain, lekin practically in-person application fast hoti hai. Hum process karwate hain.
